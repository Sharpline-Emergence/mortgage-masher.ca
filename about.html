What Is MortgageMasher.ca?

MortgageMasher.ca is a free Canadian mortgage calculator designed for first-time homebuyers and experienced investors alike. It calculates exact monthly payments, CMHC mortgage insurance costs, and complete amortization schedules using Canadian mortgage formulas with semi-annual compounding as required by the Interest Act of Canada.

Unlike generic calculators, MortgageMasher.ca handles the nuances of Canadian mortgages: CMHC insurance premiums (2026 rates), maximum 25-year amortization for insured mortgages, multiple payment frequencies (monthly, semi-monthly, bi-weekly, weekly), and the full range of down payment scenarios from 5% to 20%+.

The calculator accounts for property taxes, condo/strata fees, homeowners insurance, and down payment options—giving you the complete picture of your monthly housing cost before you talk to a lender or broker.

Why MortgageMasher.ca Exists

Most Canadian mortgage calculators fall into two camps: oversimplified (missing CMHC insurance, property taxes, and condo fees) or needlessly complex (buried under jargon and extra features). MortgageMasher.ca splits the difference.

The goal is simple: help you understand your mortgage before you apply. See exactly where your payment goes. Know what CMHC will cost. Compare 25-year vs. 20-year amortization without guessing. Understand the difference between monthly and accelerated bi-weekly payments.

Armed with this information, you can shop for brokers or lenders with confidence, negotiate better terms, and make a decision that actually fits your financial situation.
