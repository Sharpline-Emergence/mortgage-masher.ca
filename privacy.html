Privacy Policy

What MortgageMasher.ca collects, what it doesn't, and how the calculator actually works under the hood.

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

The mortgage calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser. Your home price, down payment, interest rate, and every other number you enter is never sent to, or stored on, our servers — it's calculated on your device and disappears when you close the tab. The only information we ever actually receive is what you choose to type into the contact form on our Contact page.

Information We Collect

The mortgage calculator

All calculator inputs — home price, down payment, interest rate, amortization period, payment frequency, property tax, condo fees, and insurance — are processed with JavaScript in your own browser. Nothing you enter into the calculator is transmitted to, logged by, or stored on our servers.

"Email My Results"

The Email Results feature opens your own email application with a pre-filled message, using your device's standard mailto: link. The recipient address and message content go directly from your email client to whoever you send it to — they never pass through our servers, and we never see them.

Contact form

If you fill out the form on our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, and message so we can respond to you. This form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. We use this information only to reply to your inquiry and do not add you to any mailing list or share it with anyone else.

Advertising cookies

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. We don't control this data or have access to it — it's governed by Google's own privacy policy.

How Google Uses Your Data

Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising through aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google handles data across its advertising products, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The only parties who ever have access to any information are:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions on our behalf.
  • Google AdSense — serves the ads on this site and may set its own cookies, as described above.

Neither party is authorized to use information collected here for any purpose beyond what's described in this policy and their own privacy policies.

Your Rights (BC's Personal Information Protection Act)

If you're a BC resident, PIPA gives you the right to know what personal information an organization holds about you, request corrections, and ask that it be deleted. Since we only ever hold what you've submitted through our contact form, you can exercise these rights at any time by emailing us — see Contact below.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach out via our Contact page, or email hello@sharplinedigital.com.
